I’m an AI & Data Engineer at Acer EMEA, with 5+ years of combined experience across AI, data engineering, and analytics. My work spans enterprise agents, retrieval systems, MCP integrations, and Microsoft Fabric and Azure data engineering.

I enjoy taking an initiative from the first conversation to a working solution: understanding the task, choosing an architecture, building it, and making it easier to operate. That can mean an application, an automated workflow, or a better way to work with enterprise data.

My path started in civil engineering. At Politecnico di Milano, I graduated with 110 cum laude and applied neural networks and regression to urban crash data, leading to a peer-reviewed publication. Data consulting and enterprise BI led me to the AI work I do today.

Enterprise projects

Production workflow

Product content review

An agentic workflow that checks e-commerce product content against regulatory documents and internal legal guidance, with legal and e-commerce teams reviewing the reports.

LangGraph · LangChain · Hybrid retrieval

Approach & contribution
The need
Help teams identify product claims that need attention and keep reviewing newly added products.
My contribution
Mapped the product database with its owner, designed the retrieval and agent workflow, and implemented analysis, review/scoring, and report-formatting stages.
The approach
Combined full-text and keyword retrieval with semantic vector search over reference documents. Grounded agents in those sources and added a second agent to review the analysis.
In use
The production workflow produces reports for human review. A reusable assistant skill also supports database-driven, on-demand runs.

Production RAG

Enterprise document retrieval

Summary-based retrieval that selects relevant documents first, then inspects the original sources when an answer needs more detail.

RAG · Embeddings · Document summaries

Approach & contribution
The need
Make a collection of large, varied enterprise documents easier to search and use in answers.
My contribution
Researched and proposed the architecture, secured manager agreement, and implemented it with the team.
The approach
Embedded document summaries linked to original sources. The workflow can answer from a summary or inspect the related source documents when the summary is insufficient.
In use
The custom retrieval approach has been operating for over a year.
